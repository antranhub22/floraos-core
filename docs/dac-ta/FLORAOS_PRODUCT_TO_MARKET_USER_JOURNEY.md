# FloraOS — Product-to-Market User Journey
## Final UX / Product Specification

**Version:** 1.0  
**Product:** FloraOS  
**Core Platform:** FloraOS-core  
**Vertical:** Flower Shops / Florists  
**Primary User:** Store Owner / Shop Manager

---

## 1. Strategic Objective

FloraOS should not force a flower shop owner to learn multiple AI tools, content tools, image tools, video tools, analytics tools, and automation platforms.

The owner should be able to start with one simple intention:

> **“I have this flower product. Help me sell it.”**

FloraOS then turns one real product into a complete marketing and sales journey:

```text
PRODUCT
   ↓
UNDERSTAND
   ↓
RESEARCH
   ↓
IDEATE
   ↓
CREATE
   ↓
LAUNCH
   ↓
SELL
   ↓
MEASURE
   ↓
LEARN
   ↓
NEXT BEST ACTION
   ↺
```

Core principle:

> **1 Product → 1 AI Marketing & Sales Journey**

---

## 2. Core UX Principle

The user should think in terms of **business outcomes**, not software modules.

Do not make the owner think:

- “I need to open Content.”
- “Then open Image Generation.”
- “Then open Video.”
- “Then open Analytics.”

Instead:

> **“I want to sell this bouquet.”**

FloraOS orchestrates the required AI agents and automation behind the scenes.

# 4. Stage 01 — BRING

### Objective
Make it extremely easy to introduce a real product into FloraOS.

### User action
Upload:
- 1–3 product photos
- Optional product name
- Optional selling price
- Optional notes

### AI / Automation
- Image ingestion
- Image quality check
- Duplicate detection
- Product asset creation
- Image preprocessing

### Output
A new draft product enters the FloraOS workspace.

**UX principle:** Photo first. Data second.

---

# 7. Stage 04 — IDEATE

### Objective
Turn market intelligence into concrete content opportunities.

Generate:
- Topics
- Angles
- Hooks
- Stories
- Selling approaches

Example for **Sweet Pink — 699K**:

1. **Emotional:** “Có những người phụ nữ không cần món quà đắt tiền, chỉ cần biết bạn vẫn nhớ.”
2. **Product:** “699K nhưng nhìn như một bó hoa 1 triệu.”
3. **Occasion:** “3 kiểu hoa sinh nhật khiến cô ấy nhớ mãi.”
4. **Storytelling:** “Anh ấy đặt bó hoa này lúc 10:47 tối…”
5. **Educational:** “Tặng hoa hồng màu gì cho người yêu?”
6. **Trend:** “Pastel flower trend đang quay trở lại.”

Show topics as selectable cards with:
- Why it may work
- Target audience
- Content format
- Relevant trend
- Suggested CTA

# 25. Product Philosophy

### Less manual work
Do not make owners repeat tasks AI can reliably perform.

### Better decisions
AI should surface useful choices, not overwhelm users.

### Business outcomes
Optimize for:

> **Revenue → Profit → Retention → Efficiency**

not vanity metrics alone.

### Human control
The owner remains in control of:
- Brand
- Product
- Pricing
- Major campaigns
- Sensitive communication
- Financial decisions

### Progressive autonomy

```text
AI suggests
    ↓
AI prepares
    ↓
AI executes after approval
    ↓
AI executes within defined boundaries
```

# 26. Final Product Definition

> **FloraOS Product-to-Market Journey is a product-first AI journey that transforms one real flower product into market intelligence, content, creative assets, campaigns, customer conversations, sales, measurable business results, and continuously improving next actions.**

The fundamental user experience is:

```text
“I have this flower product.”
        ↓
“FloraOS understands it.”
        ↓
“FloraOS tells me how the market can use it.”
        ↓
“FloraOS gives me content ideas.”
        ↓
“I choose the direction.”
        ↓
“FloraOS creates the content, images and videos.”
        ↓
“FloraOS packages the campaign.”
        ↓
“AI checks everything.”
        ↓
“I approve.”
        ↓
“FloraOS publishes.”
        ↓
“AI helps convert customers.”
        ↓
“FloraOS measures revenue.”
        ↓
“FloraOS learns.”
        ↓
“FloraOS tells me what to do next.”
```

## North Star

> **The owner should be able to give FloraOS a real product and receive a complete, measurable path from product → market → content → customer → sale → learning with the minimum necessary human effort.**
